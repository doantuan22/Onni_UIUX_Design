# Composition anti-pattern review

Flag copied category anatomy without product reason: repeated equal feature cards, arbitrary stats, generic dual CTAs, decorative badges or FAQ blocks. Assess the whole grouping and task flow; substitute content-specific hierarchy or remove the redundant pattern.

For the whole-page case, apply the anti-template rule (`GENERIC_TEMPLATE_COMPOSITION`) in [pattern-selection.md](../../../skills/design-inspiration/pattern-selection.md) and choose replacements from the [Web Pattern Library](../../web-patterns/README.md).
