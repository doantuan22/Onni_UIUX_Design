# Intentional, non-generic UI review

This replaces the former standalone anti-slop checklist as the Phase 2 source of truth. It is not a style ban list. Require a product/direction rationale for high-visibility combinations; preserve explicit brand choices that pass readability, accessibility and task checks.

The concrete defaults to diagnose and replace — with their Tailwind signatures — are in the [default banlist](default-banlist.md); keeping one needs a recorded reason.

Review clusters with [ai-tell-density.md](../ai-tell-density.md), then use the targeted color, surfaces, icons, motion or composition reference. A justified purple, serif, glass surface or card may remain; several unexamined defaults should be simplified or made specific.
